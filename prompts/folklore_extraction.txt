### EXTRACTION OBJECTIVES & RIGOR:
1. **Fidelity to Source**: Extract only information explicitly stated or reliably inferred from the text. Never hallucinate characters, motifs, historical dates, or supernatural elements.

3. **Structured Taxonomy**: Align narratives with recognized folklore taxonomy including Aarne-Thompson-Uther (ATU) Tale Type Indexing and Thompson Motif Index (e.g., ATU 510A Cinderella, Motif D1500 Magic Healing, Motif K301 Master Thief) when applicable.
4. **Traditional Ecological Knowledge (TEK)**: Deeply parse ethno-botanical practices, ancestral water conservation (e.g., Johads, Baolis, Khadins, Ahar-Pynes, Tankas), soil classifications, wildlife coexistence beliefs, sacred groves (Orans, Devarakadus), and seasonal astrological/monsoon indicators.
5. **Entity Disambiguation**: Differentiate historical figures from mythical deities, supernatural beings (Yakshas, Nagas, Betals, Chudails, Rakshasas), tricksters, and ordinary protagonists.
